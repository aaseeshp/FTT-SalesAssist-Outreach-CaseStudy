DATA DICTIONARY

This file describes every field in the accompanying JSON files.
[] means one item in a list. A dot indicates a field inside a nested object.
A leading [] refers to a record in a file whose top level is a list.
Dates use YYYY-MM-DD unless a field description says otherwise.
Amounts are illustrative USD values. Null means unknown.
Missing records do not mean zero holdings, no sales or no CRM activity.

00_context.json
---------------
REFERENCE_DATE
  The reference date for this exercise, in YYYY-MM-DD format.

USERS
  List of internal SalesAssist users.

USERS[].USER_ID
  Unique identifier for an internal sales user.

USERS[].DISPLAY_NAME
  The internal user's display name.

USERS[].REGION_IDENTIFIER
  The single region assigned to this internal user.

PRODUCT_CATALOG
  List linking fund and share-class identifiers to product names.

PRODUCT_CATALOG[].PRODUCT_SHARE_CLASS_ID
  Identifier for a specific fund share class. Use it to distinguish classes of the same fund.

PRODUCT_CATALOG[].PRODUCT_ID
  Internal fund identifier shared by the fund's share classes.

PRODUCT_CATALOG[].PRODUCT_NAME
  The fund's full display name.

PRODUCT_CATALOG[].SHARE_CLASS_LABEL
  Share-class label, such as I or A.


01_advisors.json
----------------
[].ADVISOR_ID
  Unique identifier for an external Financial Advisor; links this advisor to other files.

[].DISPLAY_NAME
  The advisor's display name.

[].ROLE_TITLE
  The advisor's professional role or title.

[].EMAIL_ID
  The advisor's primary email address. 

[].ADVISOR_PARENT_FIRM_NAME
  Name of the larger organization associated with the advisor's firm.

[].ADVISOR_FIRM_NAME
  Name of the firm where the advisor currently works.

[].RELANTIONSHIP_COMMENTS
  Free-text relationship notes recorded in the advisor profile.

[].REGION_IDENTIFIER
  region to which the advisor is assigned.

[].BIRTHDAY_MONTH_DAY
  Birthday month and day in MM-DD format. No birth year is supplied; null means unknown.


02_activities.json
------------------
[].ADVISOR_ID
  Identifier of the advisor associated with this CRM activity.

[].INTERACTION_ID
  Unique identifier for the activity record.

[].INTERACTION_DATE
  Date on which the interaction occurred, in YYYY-MM-DD format.

[].INTERACTION_CHANNEL
  Kind of interaction, such as a meeting, call or email.

[].COMMUNICATION_DIRECTION
  Direction of communication: inbound from the advisor, outbound from the sales team, or two-way.

[].MEANINGFUL_CONTACT_FLAG
  Source-system flag indicating whether the interaction was marked value-added: Y or N. It is a recorded signal by the person who is logging the activity in the system, not proof of advisor interest.

[].INTERACTION_SUBJECT
  Short title or subject of the activity.

[].INTERACTION_DETAILS
  Free-text account of the discussion, requests or follow-up commitments.

[].PRODUCT_NAMES
  List of product names associated with the activity. An empty list means no product names are supplied for that record.

[].REGION_IDENTIFIER
  region associated with the activity.


03_holdings.json
----------------
[].ADVISOR_ID
  Identifier of the advisor associated with the holding.

[].PRODUCT_NAME
  Full display name of the held fund.

[].PRODUCT_SHARE_CLASS_ID
  Identifier of the specific fund share class held.

[].INVESTMENT_VEHICLE
  Product vehicle, such as a Open Ended Fund/ Closed Ended Fund / Separately managed Account (SMA) / ETF.

[].ASSET_CATEGORY
  Broad investment category, such as equity or fixed income or multi-asset or alternatives.

[].CURRENT_HOLDING_VALUE
  Reported value of this holding at REFERENCE_DATE, in illustrative USD amounts.

[].PREVIOUS_HOLDING_VALUE
  Reported value at the previous comparison point, in USD. The date of that comparison point is not supplied.

[].HOLDING_VALUE_CHANGE
  CURRENT_HOLDING_VALUE minus PREVIOUS_HOLDING_VALUE, in USD. Positive means an increase; negative means a decrease. This change alone does not establish a purchase or redemption.

[].REFERENCE_DATE
  Date of the current holdings snapshot, in YYYY-MM-DD format.


04_recent_sales.json
--------------------
[].ADVISOR_ID
  Identifier of the advisor whose recent business is summarized.

[].MOST_RECENT_TRANSACTION_DATE
  Latest reported sales or transaction date, in YYYY-MM-DD format.

[].MOST_RECENT_TRANSACTION_AMOUNT
  Reported total amount for the latest sales date, in illustrative USD amounts.

[].PREVIOUS_TRANSACTION_DATE
  Previous reported sales date, in YYYY-MM-DD format; not necessarily the previous calendar day.

[].PREVIOUS_TRANSACTION_AMOUNT
  Reported total amount for the previous sales date, in USD.

[].RECENT_TRANSACTION_DIRECTION
  Reported direction of the latest business, such as purchase or redemption.

[].TRANSACTION_DIRECTION_CHANGED
  Whether the latest reported direction differs from the previous reported direction: true means changed; false means unchanged.

[].RECENT_DAILY_TRANSACTIONS
  Object containing the latest reported day and its product breakdown.

[].RECENT_DAILY_TRANSACTIONS.TRANSACTION_SUMMARY_DATE
  Date covered by the nested daily summary, in YYYY-MM-DD format.

[].RECENT_DAILY_TRANSACTIONS.PRODUCT_SHARE_CLASS_DETAILS
  List of amounts reported for individual fund share classes on that day.

[].RECENT_DAILY_TRANSACTIONS.PRODUCT_SHARE_CLASS_DETAILS[].PRODUCT_SHARE_CLASS_ID
  Identifier of the fund share class in this breakdown item.

[].RECENT_DAILY_TRANSACTIONS.PRODUCT_SHARE_CLASS_DETAILS[].PRODUCT_NAME
  Full display name of the fund in this breakdown item.

[].RECENT_DAILY_TRANSACTIONS.PRODUCT_SHARE_CLASS_DETAILS[].TRANSACTION_AMOUNT
  Reported amount for this share class on the reporting date, in USD. Interpret it with RECENT_TRANSACTION_DIRECTION.


05_fund_reference.json
----------------------
[].REFERENCE_PRODUCT_ID
  Fund identifier used by the reference feed. It happens to match PRODUCT_ID in these files; do not assume the two identifiers are always interchangeable.

[].PRODUCT_SHARE_CLASS_ID
  Internal identifier for the specific fund share class described.

[].PRODUCT_ID
  Internal identifier for the underlying fund across share classes.

[].PRODUCT_NAME
  The fund's formal or legal name.

[].PRODUCT_CATEGORY
  Reference-feed investment category. The category labels here are fictional examples.

[].OVERALL_RATING
  Illustrative overall star rating on a 1-to-5 scale. 

[].ONE_YEAR_RETURN_PERCENT
  Illustrative return over the one-year period ending at REFERENCE_DATE, in percentage units. For example, 5.2 means 5.2 percent.

[].REFERENCE_DATE
  Date to which the reference information applies, in YYYY-MM-DD format.

[].REFERENCE_NOTE
  Explains the synthetic nature or source limitations of this record.


06_documents.json
-----------------
[].CONTENT_ID
  Unique identifier for the document metadata record.

[].PRODUCT_SHARE_CLASS_ID
  Identifier of the fund share class to which the document applies.

[].CONTENT_TYPE
  Type of material, such as a factsheet, product commentary, snapshot, prospectus or presentation.

[].RELEASE_DATE
  Date the document was published, in YYYY-MM-DD format.

[].INTENDED_AUDIENCE
  Intended audience: internal company use or external sharing.

[].REVIEW_STATUS
  Recorded approval state of the material, such as approved. Interpret it together with its audience and product identity.

[].CONTENT_URL
  Document location. All links use example.invalid and are intentionally not live.
